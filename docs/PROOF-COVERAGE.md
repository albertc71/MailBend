## Laws without a public claim

- [`awaiting_incomplete_is_unknown`](../LAWS.bend#L1921). Awaiting-reply classification keeps incomplete evidence without a positive reply unknown; this is an evidence-status contract, not a mailbox-effect or send/delete safety claim.
- [`brief_preserves_selected_uids`](../LAWS.bend#L1981). Brief classification preserves the selected UID sequence and multiplicity; this output-completeness contract is separate from the read-plans no-write claim.
- [`brief_partitions_selected_uids`](../LAWS.bend#L1988). Brief classification preserves each selected UID occurrence count; this grouping contract is separate from mailbox effects and does not assert unique UIDs in the input.
- [`awaiting_disabled_filters_keep_candidate`](../LAWS.bend#L2003). Disabling both awaiting-reply filters retains each sampled candidate; this candidate-selection contract is separate from the read-plans no-write claim.
- [`brief_complete_unseen_highwater`](../LAWS.bend#L2008). A complete unseen brief checkpoints at the window high-water UID; this pagination contract does not establish mailbox effects or runtime acquisition completeness.
- [`brief_partial_unseen_prefix`](../LAWS.bend#L2014). A partial unseen brief checkpoints at the last retained UID; this pagination contract is separate from the read-plans no-write claim.
- [`brief_arrivals_keep_prefix`](../LAWS.bend#L2020). An arrivals brief checkpoints at the last retained UID for either continuation state; this checkpoint contract is separate from mailbox-effect safety.
- [`brief_range_wire`](../LAWS.bend#L2040). The UID-range search key renders its supplied endpoints exactly; this search-selection contract is separate from the read-wire EXAMINE/BODY.PEEK guarantee.
- [`brief_completed_has_no_continuation`](../LAWS.bend#L2045). A completed brief emits a null continuation cursor; this output-pagination contract is separate from mailbox-effect safety.
- [`brief_unseen_filter_interval`](../LAWS.bend#L2052). Unseen brief filtering includes only UIDs greater than the lower boundary and at most the high-water boundary; this selection contract is separate from mailbox-effect safety.
